# Ritual

Design your day and meet your future self. A small static site: a day planner, a future-self tool, the Future Self Channel (fictional characters, their stories, and a recordable 9:16 future-self video), and the Morning Ritual (coffee, breathing, breakfast, music, reading, calendar).

Everything you choose stays in your browser. The portfolio lives separately at https://ahmedabouabass.github.io/Ahmad-Portfolio/
